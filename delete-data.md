# Delete your TradeProof data

**App:** TradeProof (package `io.github.archangel77.tradeproof`)
**Developer:** Panagiotis Panageas
**Contact:** tradeproof.app.support@gmail.com

TradeProof has **no user account and no server**. Everything the app stores — photographs,
the text read from them, clients, your business details and logo, jobs, line items and
generated invoice PDFs — is kept only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot
see it and hold no copy of it.

## How to delete your data

**1. Delete individual items inside the app**
Open a job → delete a document (the photo file is removed from the device) or delete the job
(its documents, line items and invoices are removed with it). Invoices you have already shared
as PDFs exist only in whatever app you sent them to; delete those there.

**2. Delete everything at once**
Uninstall TradeProof: **Settings → Apps → TradeProof → Uninstall**. This removes every record
and every file the app created. A reinstall starts completely empty.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

- **Deleted:** photographs and their extracted text, client names and contact details, your
  business details, IBAN, account holder and logo, jobs, line items, invoice PDFs, and the cached
  subscription status. Nothing remains on any server, because none is involved.
- **Kept by Google, not by us:** your purchase and renewal history for the
  `tradeproof_monthly` subscription, because Google Play processes the payment. That record is
  governed by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and by your Google
  Play account settings, not by the app.
- **Retention period on our side:** none — there is nothing to retain.

## Questions
